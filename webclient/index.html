Asking the gateway for transmitters…

A transmitter appears here as soon as the gateway either receives its video (multicast to 239.1.1.1, UDP 4400–4415) or gets an answer from its address (192.168.167.10–25, checked every 5 s). A transmitter whose computer is asleep sends no video, so the address check is what finds it. This page asks the gateway every 3 s.

Nothing after 10 s? The gateway's network port must be on the same network segment (VLAN) as the extenders and receive their multicast; see the README, section "Network".

Switch channel with the buttons above or Right Ctrl, Right Ctrl, then 1–16. This changes this page only; the hardware receiver keeps its own channel.
