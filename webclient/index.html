Asking the gateway for transmitters…

A transmitter appears here as soon as the gateway either receives its video (multicast to 239.1.1.1, UDP 4400–4415) or gets an answer from its address (192.168.167.10–25, checked every 5 s). A transmitter whose computer is asleep sends no video, so the address check is what finds it. This page asks the gateway every 3 s.

Nothing after 10 s? The gateway's network port must be on the same network segment (VLAN) as the extenders and receive their multicast: network requirements.

Switch channel with the buttons above or Right Ctrl, Right Ctrl, then 1–16. This changes this page only; the hardware receiver keeps its own channel. Old or new unit? TESmart FAQ

Copied text

Recognised by Tesseract on the gateway from the compressed video, so check it before use: similar characters (0 and O, 1 and l) can be mixed up.

Type text into

Each character is sent as a key press on a US keyboard layout. Line breaks press Enter. Typographic quotes and dashes become plain ones; characters with no US key (for example ä or €) are skipped and listed when done. The computer must use a US layout for symbols to come out right.
